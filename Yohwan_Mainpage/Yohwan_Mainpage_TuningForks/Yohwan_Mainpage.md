## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The main page of John Kim's high school portfolio website. It introduces the student and serves as the central hub connecting visitors to three subpages: Violin, Alchemy, and Rowing.

### 1.2 Core Concept

The page is themed around the concept of **Resonance** — the idea that music, science, culture, and identity are interconnected through unseen relationships.

This concept must be communicated visually at the highest possible level of sophistication through animations of pulses, waves, and tuning forks.

### 1.3 Design Reference

- **Primary:** Refer to `wireframes.pdf` for the visual layout and screen-by-screen breakdown.
- Figures in the wireframes are reference guides — better shapes or compositions may be substituted.
- Gray text on wireframes are instructions, not visual elements.

---

## 2. Goals

### 2.1 Primary Goals

- Create a visually striking first impression that defines the tone for the entire portfolio.
- Visually communicate the concept of "Resonance" through animation — not through text.
- Serve as a clear, intuitive navigation hub to the three subpages.

### 2.2 Out of Scope

- Detailed narrative or biographical content (reserved for subpages).
- Backend or server-side components.

### 4.1 Thematic Direction

- Should feel simultaneously scientific and artistic.
- Sophisticated animation of wave and pulse behavior — physically grounded, not decorative.

## 7. Animation Principles

- All animations powered by GSAP.
- Resonance concept must be communicated visually — avoid explaining it through text.
- Pulse, wave, and tuning fork animations should feel physically accurate and refined.
- Avoid generic motion design; aim for a distinctive visual language.
- Ensure smooth transitions into and out of any subpage navigation event.
