2023

First Strokes

Joined the novice crew team knowing nothing but the word "row." Learned the catch, the drive, the finish — and that a boat only moves when eight people commit to the exact same instant. My first regatta: we finished last, and I was hooked anyway.

2024

Finding the Rhythm

Earned a seat in the varsity eight. Mornings on the water taught me the same patience as my AP Chemistry labs: rate matters, timing matters, and small inputs compound. Took silver at the city championships and started filming & editing our race recaps.

2025

Power Ten

Named stroke seat — the rower the whole boat follows. Led a "power ten" through the regional final to a podium finish, and led a student research project on exothermic reactions: how breaking and forming bonds releases energy you can actually feel.

2026

The Sprint — Warmth

The last 300m of a race is pure chemistry: stored energy converted to heat, lungs burning, the water around the hull almost glowing. That warmth — exothermic, human, shared with eight teammates — is what I want to bring with me to college.
